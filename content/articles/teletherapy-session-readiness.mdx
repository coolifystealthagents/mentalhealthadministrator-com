## Set the teletherapy readiness scope

Check Teletherapy Session Readiness starts with a written boundary. Name the client, the source, the owner, and the evidence. A clear rule keeps routine coordination separate from clinical judgment.

The main risk is that a session can start without a working link or privacy check. Record the source and time before changing a status.

## Capture the evidence

A usable record includes client, session, link, consent, location, and owner. Keep the source beside the record so another reviewer can reconstruct what happened.

The scheduling lead confirms the decision or exception. The assistant can test the link, confirm the client, and route clinical questions, but should not fill a gap with an assumption.

## Separate preparation from judgment

Decisions about clinical and privacy decisions remain with the authorized clinical or administrative owner. Use three labels: proceed, pause for missing evidence, and escalate.

## Run the review

Review failed links, missing consent, and late starts. For example, a client cannot access the video room. Record what evidence was available and who owned the next step.

## Pilot one workflow

Start with one session type. Test the checklist on a small batch and review exceptions with the owner.
