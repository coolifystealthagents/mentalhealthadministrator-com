## Set the group roster changes scope

Govern Group Roster Changes starts with a written boundary. Name the client, the source, the owner, and the evidence. A clear rule keeps routine coordination separate from clinical judgment.

The main risk is that a client can be added or removed without the required consent. Record the source and time before changing a status.

## Capture the evidence

A usable record includes client, group, change, date, consent, owner, and status. Keep the source beside the record so another reviewer can reconstruct what happened.

The group coordinator confirms the decision or exception. The assistant can record the change, check consent, and route clinical questions, but should not fill a gap with an assumption.

## Separate preparation from judgment

Decisions about clinical and group decisions remain with the authorized clinical or administrative owner. Use three labels: proceed, pause for missing evidence, and escalate.

## Run the review

Review unapproved changes, missing consent, and communication gaps. For example, a client is moved to a different group. Record what evidence was available and who owned the next step.

## Pilot one workflow

Start with one group. Test the checklist on a small batch and review exceptions with the owner.
