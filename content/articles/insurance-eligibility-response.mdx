## Set the eligibility responses scope

Track Insurance Eligibility Responses starts with a written boundary. Name the client, the source, the owner, and the evidence. A clear rule keeps routine coordination separate from clinical judgment.

The main risk is that an eligibility result can be reused after coverage changes. Record the source and time before changing a status.

## Capture the evidence

A usable record includes client, payer, plan, check date, source, result, and exception. Keep the source beside the record so another reviewer can reconstruct what happened.

The billing lead confirms the decision or exception. The assistant can check the source, record the result, and route coverage questions, but should not fill a gap with an assumption.

## Separate preparation from judgment

Decisions about coverage and clinical decisions remain with the authorized clinical or administrative owner. Use three labels: proceed, pause for missing evidence, and escalate.

## Run the review

Review stale checks, missing sources, and exceptions. For example, a payer portal shows inactive coverage. Record what evidence was available and who owned the next step.

## Pilot one workflow

Start with one payer. Test the checklist on a small batch and review exceptions with the owner.
