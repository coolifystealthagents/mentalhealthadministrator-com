## How to approach: Check a discharge summary against the aftercare plan

In the discharge summary check workflow, a client discharge with an aftercare plan arrives before anyone confirms the client, the period, or the responsible queue. ## Set the decision boundary for the clinical director

The clinical director approves a discharge summary check exception, while the preparer records the evidence and the time of the handoff. ## Record and release the exception
