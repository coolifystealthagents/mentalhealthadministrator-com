## Set the claim status scope

Run a Mental Health Claim Status Queue starts with a written boundary. Name the client, the source, the owner, and the evidence. A clear rule keeps routine coordination separate from clinical judgment.

The main risk is that a claim can remain pending without an owner. Record the source and time before changing a status.

## Capture the evidence

A usable record includes claim, payer, status, checked date, next action, and owner. Keep the source beside the record so another reviewer can reconstruct what happened.

The billing lead confirms the decision or exception. The assistant can check status, record the result, and route appeals, but should not fill a gap with an assumption.

## Separate preparation from judgment

Decisions about coding and appeal decisions remain with the authorized clinical or administrative owner. Use three labels: proceed, pause for missing evidence, and escalate.

## Run the review

Review stale status, denials, and open appeals. For example, a claim shows pending for 30 days. Record what evidence was available and who owned the next step.

## Pilot one workflow

Start with one payer. Test the checklist on a small batch and review exceptions with the owner.
