One referral may arrive as a fax from a primary care office, a portal upload from the person, and a later phone call. Reconciliation should connect those arrivals without erasing who sent what or asking an administrator to decide whether the person is clinically suitable for a service.

## Quick overview:

- Give every arrival its own source event before considering a merge.
- Match using approved identifiers and route ambiguous identities for review.
- Preserve differing service requests, contact details, attachments, and timestamps.
- Designate one current referral record while retaining links to all source events.
- Keep eligibility, urgency, clinical fit, and acceptance decisions with authorized personnel.

## Separate arrival, referral, and person records

These objects answer different questions. An arrival records what entered a channel and when. A referral groups related material into one administrative handoff. A person record identifies who may receive services. Combining them at ingestion makes it easy to attach a document to the wrong person or overwrite an earlier request.

For each arrival, capture source-event ID, channel, received timestamp, sender organization or person as stated, source reference, intended recipient, stated service request, attachment count, identity-match state, and storage location. Keep the original artifact intact. Avoid entering sensitive narrative in a routing comment.

The HHS [treatment disclosure guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/481/does-hipaa-permit-health-care-providers-to-share-information-for-treatment-purposes/index.html) provides context for information sharing between health care providers for treatment. A practice still needs approved authority, identity, routing, and minimum-information procedures for each referral workflow.

## Match conservatively

Use an approved combination of identifiers rather than one convenient field. Similar names, changed phone numbers, and mistyped birth dates create false matches. A strong match can link the arrival to an existing person and referral; a possible match should enter an identity exception queue.

Do not create a permanent person record simply to clear an inbox if required identifiers are absent. A temporary referral ID can preserve custody while clarification occurs. Record each search performed and the reason for the match state so another worker does not repeat the same uncertain search.

## Reconcile facts without choosing a winner

Two sources may disagree about address, insurance, requested clinician, appointment preference, or referring organization. Preserve each value with provenance. An administrative rule may identify a designated demographic source, but it should not be extended to clinical or legal questions.

Use a comparison table in the referral record:

| Element | Source A | Source B | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| contact number | value and receipt time | value and receipt time | verification pending |
| requested service | exact stated category | exact stated category | authorized review |
| referring office | sender details | absent | retained from source A |
| contact permission | stated instruction | conflicting instruction | outreach paused |

The result column describes the next administrative state. It does not declare one source truthful.

## Keep the handoff chronology

A referral history should show received, indexed, matched, clarification requested, routed for protected review, accepted or otherwise dispositioned by an authorized person, outreach assigned, and closed. Store the actor, time, and evidence for each transition.

Suppose a fax requests medication management, while a portal submission on the same day asks about therapy and lists a different phone number. Link the arrivals after an approved identity match, retain both requested services, and pause contact if the permission or destination is uncertain. The clinical reviewer determines fit; the proper administrative or privacy owner resolves the contact conflict.

If an external sender calls for status, staff should use a response script and authority rule approved by the practice. Receipt of a referral does not automatically authorize disclosure of the person's status.

## Detect source-specific failures

Reconciliation data can expose broken intake channels. Review arrivals missing pages, faxes routed to the wrong workgroup, portal submissions without required identifiers, and repeat calls that cite no acknowledgment. Group problems by source and channel before changing forms or instructions.

Useful counts include arrivals, unique referral records, linked duplicates, unresolved identity conflicts, referrals with no current owner, and source events lacking an acknowledgment result. Define the counting unit, because three arrivals may represent one referral. These are workflow indicators and do not measure clinical access or outcomes.

A sample review should begin with raw fax or portal queues and trace forward, not only sample records already in the referral system. Otherwise, entirely missed arrivals remain invisible.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should two arrivals be merged?

Link them after the approved identity and referral criteria are met. Preserve both source events and their timestamps; merging should not destroy provenance.

### Can staff decide which requested service is correct?

No. Staff may record the exact requests and apply an approved administrative routing rule. Clinical fit, urgency, and acceptance belong to qualified practice personnel.

### What if the referrer and prospective patient give different contact instructions?

Preserve both instructions, pause any action not clearly authorized, and route the conflict according to the practice's communication and privacy procedure.

## Preserve the route into the practice

A reconciled referral is not a flattened summary. It is a connected history of source events, identity work, conflicts, and authorized decisions. Feed verified documentary gaps into the [intake document exception queue](/blog/mental-health-intake-document-exception-queue), and apply [waitlist contact preferences](/blog/behavioral-health-waitlist-contact-preferences) before outreach. Good reconciliation gives reviewers the complete handoff without turning clerical matching into a clinical decision.
