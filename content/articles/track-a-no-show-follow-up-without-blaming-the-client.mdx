## How to approach: Track a no-show follow-up without blaming the client

A no-show follow-up should learn what happened and offer a next step, not assign blame. Start with the date, the appointment, and the contact attempt. A note that says client no-showed helps nobody. Write the deadline as a date and a local time. The records release should name the authority and the scope. The schedule change should be checked against the room and the billing.

Keep the follow-up short and respectful. The client may be embarrassed, overwhelmed, or unable to attend. The script should make it easy to return. ## Record the attempt and the barrier

Record the channel, the time, and the outcome of each attempt. If the client says transport was the problem, record the barrier in their words. Do not assume the reason. ## Offer the next step
