# Route Cross-Team Mental Health Practice Administration Escalations to One Owner

Published: October 27, 2026

## Why this control matters

In Mental Health Administrator's virtual assistant operations workflow: A mental health practice administration issue often crosses teams: the specialist can see the problem, but the fix belongs to access, engineering, billing, or a vendor. When every escalation is sent to a group channel, it is read by many people and owned by none. Route Cross-Team Mental Health Practice Administration Escalations to One Owner turns that situation into a bounded workflow with a source, one accountable owner, and a recorded close. The reviewer checks the source, the named owner, and the next action before the item leaves the queue.
