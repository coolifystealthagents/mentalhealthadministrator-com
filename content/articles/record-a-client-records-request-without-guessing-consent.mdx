## How to approach: Record a client records request without guessing consent

A records request should be built from verified authority, not from an urgent email. Start with the requester, the client, the date range, and the permitted use. A request that is processed before verification is a privacy risk. Ask whether a new reviewer could act on the record without a conversation. The client's preference should be recorded and followed. The team should test the script with a real call type.

Keep the request and the verification together. A later reviewer should see who asked, what authority was checked, and what was released. Keep the evidence with the record rather than in a separate log. Record the exception and the person who approved it. The review should end with one owner and one date. The front desk should record the caller's relationship before sharing any detail.

## Verify the requester and the authority

Record the requester's name, relationship, and organization. Verify the request through an approved channel. A reply to an unsolicited email is not verification. ## List the records and the date range
