## How to approach: Record a client records request without guessing consent

A records request should be built from verified authority, not from an urgent email. Start with the requester, the client, the date range, and the permitted use. A request that is processed before verification is a privacy risk. Ask whether a new reviewer could act on the record without a conversation.

Keep the request and the verification together. A later reviewer should see who asked, what authority was checked, and what was released. Keep the evidence with the record rather than in a separate log. Record the exception and the person who approved it.

## Verify the requester and the authority

Record the requester's name, relationship, and organization. Verify the request through an approved channel. A reply to an unsolicited email is not verification. A control that depends on memory is not yet a control.

Check the authorization or the legal basis for the release. When the authority is unclear, stop and route the request to the privacy owner. Separate what was observed from what was assumed. Update the template when the same question appears twice.

## List the records and the date range

Write the records requested and the date range. A request for all records is broader than most authorizations allow. Do not expand the scope to be helpful. Separate what was observed from what was assumed.

Mark each record as included, pending, or excluded with the reason. The packet should show what was not found. Keep the boundary visible in the place where the work is assigned. Keep the evidence with the record rather than in a separate log.

## Check the permitted use and the release

Record the permitted use and any limit. Do not share the records with a second party because it is convenient. Apply the minimum necessary standard. Record the exception and the person who approved it.

Record the date, the recipient, the method, and the confirmation. Use a secure channel and keep the delivery record. Record the date and the source so the next reviewer can repeat the check. Separate what was observed from what was assumed.

## Review the request before release

Ask a second reviewer to check the requester, the authority, the date range, and the redactions. A short checklist is more reliable than memory. Update the template when the same question appears twice. Record the date and the source so the next reviewer can repeat the check.

File the confirmation with the packet. A request that cannot be reconstructed later is not controlled. A control that depends on memory is not yet a control. Keep the boundary visible in the place where the work is assigned.
