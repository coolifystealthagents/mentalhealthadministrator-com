## How to approach: Keep a clinician schedule change from breaking the week

A clinician schedule change should be checked against the clients, the rooms, and the billing before it is final. Start with the sessions that would be affected. A change that is made without checking the clients will create a wave of rescheduling. Name the owner in the same line as the action.

Keep the process short enough to use during a busy week. The practice manager should be able to approve a change in one conversation. The record should show what was checked. Update the template when the same question appears twice.

## Check the affected sessions

List the sessions that would move and the clients who need a new time. Prioritize the clients who cannot easily reschedule. A client with a standing appointment may need a different solution. Separate what was observed from what was assumed.

Record the reason for the change and the notice given. A change with short notice may require a phone call, not just a portal message. If the source is unclear, route the question instead of guessing. Write the deadline as a date and a local time.

## Check the rooms and the billing

Confirm that the new time has a room and the right equipment. A telehealth session needs a platform and a private space. The change should not create a room conflict. A short note with four fields is easier to use than a long narrative.

Check the billing and the authorization. A session that moves to a new date may fall outside an authorization. The billing team should know before the session. Record the exception and the person who approved it.

## Communicate the change

Send the client a clear message with the new time and the reason. Use the client's preferred channel. A message that only says the appointment changed creates a callback. Name the owner in the same line as the action.

Record the communication and the client's response. A client who cannot attend the new time should be offered an alternative. Use the same format for every handover so the next reader can scan it. Record the date and the source so the next reviewer can repeat the check.

## Review the change after the week

Review the sessions that moved and the clients who missed. Look for a pattern in the scheduling or the communication. Update the process. Use the same format for every handover so the next reader can scan it.

Keep the change note with the schedule. A later review may need to see what was decided. The goal is a stable week, not a perfect schedule. Review the first few notes together before the process expands.
