## Set the decision boundary for the clinical director

The clinical director approves a supervision record exception, while the preparer records the evidence and the time of the handoff. ## Record and release the exception

When the supervision record is incomplete, the supervision record case pauses until the supervision file shows a corrected value and a named releaser.
