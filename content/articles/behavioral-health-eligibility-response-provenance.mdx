## Separate facts from protected decisions

For eligibility response provenance, an assistant may record what an approved source states, compare named administrative fields, send a current template, and route an exception. The assistant should not convert a questionnaire into screening, infer clinical fit, select a diagnosis or code, determine medical necessity, decide disclosure authority, or interpret payer language as a guarantee.

For eligibility response provenance, preserve the original wording when a message includes a clinical, safety, medication, legal, or complaint issue. Stop the routine script and transfer it under the practice procedure. The transfer log proves that a handoff was attempted; it does not prove that the issue was resolved.

## Build a controlled operating sequence

1. **Confirm the current procedure.** Check its owner, version, effective date, and affected systems.
2. **Verify the record identity.** Use approved identifiers and pause possible duplicates for authorized review.
3. **Capture source facts.** Record values, timestamps, reference numbers, and missing evidence without inference.
4. **Apply the permitted action.** Use the approved status, template, queue, or administrative transaction.
5. **Route exceptions.** Preserve the incoming wording and identify the receiving owner and transfer time.
6. **Reconcile the result.** Compare the action with the source system and document any disagreement.
7. **Close or hand off.** Require a supported closure reason or a named owner with a next review date.

## Record fields that make work reviewable

For eligibility response provenance, use a stable local identifier, received time, source, current status, status reason, assigned owner, next action date, action history, and last reconciliation time. When external systems are involved, retain the response date, reference number, captured wording, and the person who reviewed the exception.

For eligibility response provenance, unknown, unavailable, pending, and not applicable need separate meanings. Leaving a value unknown is safer than filling a required field with an assumption. Corrections should preserve the prior value, the source that supports the change, the author, and the time.

## Quality review checklist

- Compare a defined sample with its named source.
- Confirm that status values meet their written criteria.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an assistant make clinical decisions in this workflow?

For eligibility response provenance, no. The role is limited to approved administrative actions. Assessment, diagnosis, risk evaluation, treatment, medication, and clinical prioritization remain with qualified practice personnel.

### Does a complete record prove that its contents are accurate?

For eligibility response provenance, no. Completeness means defined fields contain permitted values. Accuracy requires comparison with the named source, and some conclusions still require authorized professional judgment.

### What happens when the source systems disagree?

Preserve both values, their sources, and capture times. Route the discrepancy to the named owner rather than selecting the most convenient answer.

### How often should the process be reviewed?

Use a cadence selected by the practice and also review after system, staffing, policy, payer, service, or recurring exception changes.
