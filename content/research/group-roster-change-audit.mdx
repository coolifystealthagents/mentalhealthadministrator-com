## Research question

A method for testing group membership, consent, and communication.

## Population and evidence

Define the population from a fixed export and record the query date, filters, source, and exclusions.

## Method

Apply a short checklist and test it on a calibration sample. Separate preparation from judgment.

## Limitations

The review describes the sampled records, not the whole population. A closed status is not proof of resolution.

## Practical use

Use one bounded review to improve a workflow, then retest. Group roster review requires the change, consent, owner, and communication evidence.
