Eligibility verification is a snapshot of information returned for a particular inquiry. It is not a promise that a claim will be paid or that a quoted amount will become final client responsibility. A reproducible local review preserves the exact request context and avoids translating a complex response into a single verified checkbox.

This record concerns administrative evidence. It does not determine medical necessity, choose codes, obtain authorization, interpret a plan contract, or provide a financial guarantee.

## Question and unit of analysis

The unit is a request-response pair for a member, payer, provider context, service context, and relevant date or date range. A new request after a plan change, new calendar period, different location, or changed service context is a separate observation. Link related inquiries but do not overwrite the earlier response.

The research question is whether the practice can reconstruct what was asked, what the payer or intermediary returned, when it was returned, which fields remained ambiguous, and what communication followed. Define included appointment types and verification checkpoints before measuring completion.

For a local review, draw appointment-linked inquiries from a closed period and retain raw electronic payloads or approved portal or phone evidence. Compare structured fields with the source response. Include failed and unanswered inquiries rather than sampling only completed verifications.

> **Liftable statistic:** CMS identifies ASC X12N 270 and 271 as the eligibility inquiry and response transaction standards for covered entities using the transaction in scope. A successful response does not guarantee authorization, medical necessity, payment, or final client responsibility.

## Five evidence dimensions

Each inquiry should preserve identity match, coverage period, benefit context, network or provider context when returned, and response provenance. Identity match records whether the payer located a member, not whether all other fields are correct. Coverage period needs explicit dates and the service date being checked.

Benefit context includes the service category and any returned limitation, copayment, coinsurance, deductible, or accumulator information. Record values as payer-reported and dated. Do not calculate a guaranteed amount from incomplete accumulators or assume a broad category applies to the intended service.

Network context must identify the provider, entity, location, and product supplied in the request. If the response does not answer that question, use not returned. Active coverage and network status are separate concepts.

Provenance includes transaction ID, source, response time, raw code, human contact reference when applicable, and staff capture time. Phone calls should record whom or which department supplied the information and the questions asked, without unnecessary narrative.

## Status design

Use requested, response received, member not found, active returned, inactive returned, partial or ambiguous, payer unavailable, and escalated as evidence states. A local ready label may summarize workflow readiness, but it should not replace the underlying response.

Authorization required, authorization not required, and authorization unknown must remain distinct from eligibility. Similarly, a payer estimate, a practice estimate, an adjudicated claim, and a client payment are different financial events.

When sources conflict, keep both responses. Later information may supersede a workflow assumption prospectively, but it should not erase what was communicated earlier. Corrections should link to the original inquiry and identify the reason.

## Measures and denominators

Inquiry completion is appointment contexts with a qualifying response divided by contexts due for verification. Response completeness is returned elements divided by elements the method expected and the channel could provide. Do not penalize a standard transaction for fields it was not designed or configured to return without describing that limitation.

Report inquiries with member-not-found results, payer outages, manual follow-up, conflicting responses, and missing raw evidence. Recheck rate should use only inquiries meeting a declared trigger, such as elapsed time, plan change, reschedule across a coverage boundary, or inconsistent information.

Measure communication traceability as client-facing estimates linked to a dated payer source and approved caveat divided by sampled estimates. This assesses documentation, not whether the eventual adjudicated amount matches.

## Quality assurance

Trace a sample from appointment to request, raw response, structured summary, and communication. In reverse, select raw failures and confirm they entered the exception queue. Test transformations of dates, accumulator units, service types, and network indicators.

Common errors include checking the wrong product, using a subscriber response for the wrong dependent, carrying January information into a later service date, omitting the billing entity, and treating portal display time as plan effective time. Shared screenshots without query context are weak evidence.

## Limitations

Payer responses vary by channel and may be incomplete, delayed, or later corrected. Plan language and provider relationships can be complex. Calendar-year changes and accumulators create rapid staleness. A clean transaction may still contain information insufficient for a specific service. Local review cannot establish coverage, authorization, claim payment, contract interpretation, or client responsibility.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does active coverage mean the planned service is covered?

No. Active coverage is one scoped response element. Benefits, exclusions, network context, authorization, coding, and adjudication remain separate.

### How long is an eligibility response valid?

There is no universal validity period. Record its response time and service-date context, then define recheck triggers based on applicable policy, risk, and change signals.

### Should phone verification replace the electronic response?

Keep both as attributed evidence. A phone contact may clarify an ambiguity, but its scope, time, questions, and reference should remain visible.

### Can staff quote exact client responsibility?

Only under approved practice policy with appropriate caveats and source attribution. An eligibility response or estimate is not final adjudication.

## Conclusion

Eligibility evidence is strongest as a preserved request-response pair with five contextual dimensions. Explicit unknown states, separate authorization fields, trigger-based rechecks, and source-linked communications reduce false certainty. The result supports administration while leaving coverage and payment determinations with their authorized sources.
