Credential administration repeatedly copies facts among provider forms, licenses, identifier records, enrollment applications, roster spreadsheets, and payer portals. A value can look current while its source and effective date have disappeared. Provenance is the ability to reconstruct where a value came from, who supplied or verified it, how it changed, and where it was sent.

This record addresses evidence custody, not whether a clinician is qualified, credentialed, enrolled, or permitted to practice. Those determinations belong to the responsible organizations and authorized professionals.

## Research scope

The observation unit is a material provider-field version. Examples include legal name, practice address, license identifier, taxonomy selection, reassignment relationship, directory phone, and enrollment effective date. Different fields can have different authoritative sources. A single provider-level verified checkbox is therefore too coarse.

The research question is whether sampled values can be traced backward to evidence and forward to downstream submissions. Freeze the included providers, fields, destinations, and as-of date. Keep terminated and inactive records when they were active during the study window.

Build a field catalog, then select a reproducible sample across provider type, destination, field family, and change status. A reviewer attempts to reconstruct each sampled value using retained evidence. Record failure before requesting missing material so remediation does not rewrite the baseline.

> **Liftable statistic:** This editorial model proposes 6 provenance elements for each material field: source identity, source date, capture time, actor, transformation, and authority role. Six is a local control count, not an external requirement.

## Four evidence roles

Keep provider assertion, independent source observation, organization approval, and payer response distinct. A signed attestation shows what was asserted at a time. A licensing-board lookup is a separate observation. Internal approval records an organizational decision. A payer acceptance or effective date reports that payer's action. None should silently stand in for another.

Source precedence must be field-specific and approved. The NPI record may identify an NPI, but possession of an NPI does not prove licensure, specialty competence, payer participation, or open-panel status. Likewise, a payer portal response does not validate unrelated fields in the internal profile.

## Provenance record design

For each value, store the source artifact or durable reference, its issue or effective date, capture timestamp and time zone, actor or automated process, any normalization, and the role the source plays. Transformations include abbreviating an address, converting dates, selecting a portal option, or combining names. Retain both input and output.

Corrections should append a new version with a reason and effective point. Do not overwrite the value used in an earlier submission. Link outbound roster row, portal confirmation, or transaction reference to the exact internal versions exported. This permits forward tracing when a source changes and backward tracing when a payer questions a submission.

Unknown, not requested, pending provider response, source unavailable, and conflict are different states. A conflict record should identify both values, sources, dates, decision owner, and disposition. Administrative staff may flag and route discrepancies without choosing a professional fact they are not authorized to decide.

## Measures

Reconstructability rate is sampled values with all required provenance elements divided by sampled values to which those elements apply. Source agreement is sampled values matching the declared reference after normalization divided by values with a retrievable reference. Report missing-source and conflicting-source counts separately.

Forward traceability measures changed source values that can be linked to every in-scope destination. Correction latency begins when the practice receives qualifying evidence and ends at a defined internal update or destination confirmation. Publish both raw elapsed time and any documented pauses.

Completeness is not correctness. A fully populated provenance record can trace an incorrect assertion perfectly. Conversely, a correct-looking value without evidence is not reconstructable. Report these dimensions independently.

## Audit procedure and failure modes

Start from internal fields and trace backward. Next start from outbound submissions and trace to exact field versions. Finally select source changes and identify every affected destination. These three paths detect different gaps.

Frequent failures include undated screenshots, shared portal credentials, spreadsheets that replace prior rows, source documents saved under ambiguous names, transformations performed only in email, and payer confirmations detached from the submitted version. Bulk imports can create false uniform timestamps that reflect migration rather than effective dates. Label migrations explicitly.

Access should follow job role. Protect documents containing personal identifiers, use approved repositories, log exports, and remove access during offboarding. A provenance index may contain links and metadata rather than duplicating every sensitive artifact.

## Limitations

Some external systems do not expose durable confirmation IDs or historical screens. Source authorities can disagree, and legal or contractual rules can alter which source controls. A sampled audit may miss rare fields and destinations. High traceability does not establish qualification, enrollment, network participation, directory accuracy, or compliance. Results also depend on the field catalog and as-of date, so trend comparisons require versioned definitions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an attestation independent verification?

No. It is evidence of an assertion. Record independent checks separately and do not imply that every field requires the same verification method.

### Which system should be the single source of truth?

That phrase can obscure field-specific authority. Name an authoritative source and owner for each field, plus rules for conflicts and temporary values.

### Should old credential values be deleted?

Not when they support prior submissions or decisions. Retain them under approved retention and access policies, with effective and superseded dates.

### Can provenance prove payer enrollment?

It can trace payer evidence supporting a recorded status. It cannot extend that response beyond its provider, entity, location, plan, service, and effective period.

## Conclusion

Credential provenance is strongest at field level. Separating assertion, observation, approval, and payer response prevents evidence from acquiring authority it never had. Versioned values, explicit transformations, and bidirectional tracing let a practice repair downstream records without claiming that traceability itself proves professional status.
