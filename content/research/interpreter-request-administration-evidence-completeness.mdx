Interpreter request evidence travels across intake forms, scheduling notes, vendor portals, confirmations, and appointment records. A single checked box cannot establish the full chain. Research should distinguish what the person requested, what the practice arranged, what a vendor acknowledged, and what occurred at the encounter.

This protocol evaluates administrative evidence. It does not assess language proficiency, clinical communication quality, legal compliance, or interpreter competence. Applicable duties depend on the organization and circumstances and require qualified review.

## Research question and observation unit

The primary question is: among appointments for which language assistance was requested or administratively indicated under the approved process, how often can the request and resulting arrangement be reconstructed from source records?

Use one appointment-interpreter request pair as the unit. One appointment may have more than one request if modalities or languages change, so retain version history and identify the final applicable request. Define whether cancellations before arrangement, same-day requests, recurring appointments, and requests supplied by a representative are eligible. Never infer a request from name, country, accent, diagnosis, or a language used on a previous visit.

Build the population from both request sources and vendor or internal service logs. Using only a vendor file excludes requests that never reached the vendor and inflates apparent fulfillment. Deduplicate with appointment ID, request timestamp, language, and source, while retaining uncertain matches.

## Evidence model

Reviewers need explicit source precedence and null states.

| Element | Preferred evidence | Distinct nulls |
|---|---|---|
| Requested language | person's or authorized representative's recorded statement | unknown, not asked, declined |
| Modality or aid | recorded communication request | unspecified, unavailable |
| Appointment link | stable appointment and location ID | unmatched, appointment removed |
| Arrangement | vendor order or assigned internal resource | pending, not requested, failed |
| Confirmation | named service acknowledgment and time | awaiting, rejected, expired |
| Encounter result | operational arrival or cancellation record | not observed, appointment cancelled |

The first five rows are the core completeness elements. Encounter result is a separate follow-through observation and is not included in the five-element completeness count.

A translation request, spoken-language interpreter, sign-language interpreter, captioning, and another communication aid should not be collapsed into one generic yes value. Administrators can capture the request accurately but should not decide which accommodation is legally or clinically sufficient.

> **Liftable statistic:** A proposed local completeness review checks 5 core evidence elements: stated language, requested modality, appointment link, arrangement status, and confirmation source. Five is a study design choice, not a statutory minimum or national benchmark.

## Sampling and reviewer procedure

Freeze a reporting window and take immutable exports from intake, scheduling, and service-arrangement sources. Record query logic, time zone, extraction time, software version, and excluded counts. A census is preferable for small populations. For sampling, stratify by language, location, request channel, appointment mode, and month, while avoiding strata so small that people could be identified in publication.

Create a blinded review packet with the minimum information needed. Two reviewers independently score a calibration set as present and supported, present but conflicting, absent, not applicable under the dictionary, or source unavailable. Calculate agreement for each field and discuss discrepancies before full review. Do not convert disagreement into missingness; it may reveal an ambiguous definition.

Trace a subset from the request forward and another subset from service confirmations backward. Bidirectional tracing exposes orphaned requests and vendor records that cannot be linked to an appointment. Preserve raw values and apply normalized labels only in the analytic copy.

## Measures and denominators

The **request completeness rate** is eligible requests containing all applicable request-side fields divided by eligible reviewed requests. The **arrangement traceability rate** is requests requiring an arrangement with a linked order or internal assignment divided by reviewed requests requiring arrangement. These denominators must not be merged.

Additional measures include:

* confirmations linked to a valid appointment divided by confirmations reviewed;
* requests with conflicting language or modality across sources divided by requests with multiple available sources;
* arrangements still pending at a declared cutoff, shown in aging bands;
* encounters with an operational result recorded divided by eligible completed or cancelled appointments;
* records excluded because every source was unavailable, shown as a count rather than treated as failures.

For timeliness, choose start and stop events in advance. Request-to-order time measures an internal handoff. Order-to-confirmation time includes vendor response. Pause rules and business hours must be stated. A fast order is not proof that assistance was suitable or delivered.

## Error patterns worth investigating

Free-text language names can fragment one language into many spellings. Mapping may improve analysis, but retain the original value and have language-access leadership approve categories. Default values can create apparent completeness even when nobody asked. Compare creation timestamps and repeated patterns to identify defaults for review.

Recurring appointment series create another trap: an interpreter may be confirmed for one occurrence but copied to all. Validate at the occurrence level. Rescheduled visits need a new linkage check because location and time may change. When the vendor rejects a request, record the exact returned status and next owner instead of retaining booked.

A service-arrived field may be entered by staff without an external source. Classify it according to the evidence dictionary rather than silently treating it as confirmation. If the person reports that the arrangement did not meet the request, preserve that report and route it to the designated owner without asking an analyst to adjudicate service quality.

## Privacy, governance, and limitations

Language and disability-related information can increase reidentification risk in small groups. Limit extracts, suppress small published cells when appropriate, separate linkage files, and set deletion dates. Vendor access and transfer methods should follow approved agreements and security review.

The study misses verbal requests that were never recorded. Vendor logs may use different clocks or identifiers. A completed appointment does not prove effective communication. A missing confirmation can reflect absent service, failed integration, or incomplete documentation. Results from one period may not generalize after staff, vendor, or scheduling changes.

Administrative completeness is not a civil-rights determination. The authoritative guidance helps leaders frame obligations, but a local field set cannot certify them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is preferred language the same as interpreter requested?

No. Record them separately. A person may state a preferred language without requesting a specific service, and the applicable process may require additional authorized review.

### Can family-member interpretation be marked fulfilled?

Do not invent a universal classification. Preserve what was requested and what occurred, then apply the organization's approved policy and qualified determination. Avoid relabeling an informal arrangement as a qualified service.

### How should cancelled appointments enter the denominator?

Specify whether cancellation occurred before or after an arrangement was required. Show cancellations separately so they do not falsely appear as successful or failed fulfillment.

### What does a vendor confirmation prove?

It proves the vendor issued the recorded acknowledgment for the linked request. It does not prove arrival, identity, competence, effective communication, or satisfaction.

## Conclusion

Interpreter request research is strongest when it preserves separate evidence for stated language, requested modality, appointment linkage, arrangement, and confirmation. Encounter result remains a separate follow-through observation. Bidirectional tracing, occurrence-level matching, and honest null states reveal where the chain breaks without making unsupported claims about communication quality or compliance.
