A directory search produces leads, not confirmed placements. A listing may be current yet irrelevant to a particular product, location, modality, language request, age range, or availability need. Conversely, a failed phone call does not prove that every field in the listing is wrong. Referral checks need evidence states narrow enough to represent what was actually observed.

This research record addresses nonclinical search administration. Staff should not infer diagnosis, urgency, treatment fit, coverage, or network status beyond attributed evidence.

## Research scope

The observation unit is a referral lead generated for a declared search request. Record the directory source, query parameters, provider-location result, and observation time. Link later calls or portal inquiries as separate events. This preserves the difference between listed, contact attempted, reached, and availability described.

A search request can include administrative constraints supplied by the requester, such as geographic area, telehealth preference, payer and product, language, accessibility need, and acceptable contact method. Clinical requirements should come from qualified personnel and remain attributed, not interpreted by an assistant.

> **Liftable statistic:** CMS stated that its 2024 final rule requires applicable Medicare Advantage plans to update provider directory information within 30 days after receiving notice of a change. This scoped requirement does not prove that every directory entry is current or that a provider accepts a particular referral.

## Six evidence states

Use listed, contact attempted, contact reached, administrative criteria confirmed, appointment pathway offered, and unresolved as separate states. A lead can progress through several states, so retain an event history rather than only the latest label. Add declined or unavailable only when the source and scope are recorded.

Contact reached means the intended organization or provider responded through an approved channel. It does not mean the responder verified network participation or clinical fit. Administrative criteria confirmed should list the exact criteria and effective date. Appointment pathway offered means a concrete next step was communicated, not that an appointment was booked.

Record unreachable when attempts meet the locally declared rule. Wrong number, disconnected line, full voicemail, no response, and inaccessible portal are different failure modes. They support different directory feedback and should not be merged.

## Search packet design

A reproducible packet includes request ID, requested administrative constraints, source directory and product, query date, search terms or filters, displayed results, direct-contact events, attributed responses, and disposition. Avoid copying diagnosis or narrative into search trackers when structured administrative constraints suffice.

State whether information came from the directory, provider office, payer representative, client, or internal staff. Include capture times because panel and appointment availability can change quickly. If the payer and office conflict, present the conflict rather than resolving coverage by assumption.

For accessibility or language information, record the source's exact claim. A directory language field may not establish that a qualified language service is available for every interaction. An accessible building claim may not address communication format, sensory access, or telehealth technology.

## Measures

Lead contactability is leads with successful organizational contact divided by leads for which the contact-attempt protocol ended. Usable-lead yield is leads meeting the prespecified administrative criteria divided by unique leads checked. Search resolution is requests with an offered next pathway or documented return to the authorized owner divided by requests whose search window closed.

Report median and distribution of attempts per unique lead, duplicate-result rate, wrong-contact rate, and conflict rate between directory and direct response. For client-facing usefulness, count search packets with source dates and clear uncertainty statements. Do not call a referral successful solely because a list was sent.

Deduplicate at the provider-location level, not by name alone. One clinician at two locations may yield meaningfully different access options. Conversely, aliases and group listings can inflate counts unless linkage rules are declared.

## Communication and escalation boundaries

Explain the source and date when sharing a lead. Avoid phrases such as covered, accepting, or appropriate unless directly supported and carefully scoped. Encourage confirmation through approved payer and provider channels without transferring the entire verification burden invisibly to the client.

A routine referral queue is not an emergency service. If a communication suggests urgent or safety-related needs, follow the practice's approved escalation path and preserve the original message. Administrative metrics should record transfer events without judging clinical resolution.

## Limitations

Directory observations are time-bound and search interfaces may vary by location, account, and product. Office responses can be incomplete or change after contact. Nonresponse is ambiguous. A sample of completed searches may exclude abandoned or unlogged work and overstate reliability. A usable administrative lead does not establish clinical suitability, affordability, coverage, accommodation, or appointment completion.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a provider listed by a payer confirmed in network?

Treat the listing as dated payer-sourced evidence, not a guarantee. Product, location, service, effective date, and client-specific coverage may still require authorized confirmation.

### Should several locations for one clinician count as separate leads?

They may when location affects contact, availability, product participation, modality, or access. State the deduplication rule and show both raw and unique counts when useful.

### How should conflicting payer and provider responses be reported?

Keep both sources, wording, and timestamps. Mark the criterion unresolved and route it rather than choosing the answer that appears more favorable.

### Does an offered intake call mean the referral was completed?

No. It is an offered administrative pathway. Booking, attendance, and clinical acceptance are later and distinct events.

## Conclusion

Referral-oriented directory research should preserve the path from query to direct response. Six narrow evidence states, attributed constraints, and provider-location deduplication produce a more honest account than a list of names. The method helps locate stale leads while keeping coverage and clinical decisions with their proper owners.
