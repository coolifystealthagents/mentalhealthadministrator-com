A panel register is intended to help route inquiries toward providers who may be able to consider them. Its reliability depends on more than whether every row has an open or closed value. Status can vary by location, service, modality, age range, payer relationship, and effective date. Capacity can also change between verification and contact.

This study design evaluates administrative evidence. It does not decide clinical fit, urgency, treatment eligibility, network coverage, or whether an appointment should be offered.

## Research scope and observation unit

Define one observation as a provider-location-service relationship viewed at a specified time. Product or modality may become additional key fields where they materially change availability. Never use the provider alone as the unit if one clinician has different conditions across locations.

The target population is all relationships expected to have a panel state on the snapshot date. Include recently opened, restricted, paused, closed, and unknown records. Excluding unknown rows would inflate apparent completeness. Declare how temporary leave, future-dated changes, group practices, and shared intake pools are represented.

## Define status and restrictions

Open under stated conditions means routine inquiries within a documented service, location, modality, and referral scope are considered without an additional case-specific gate. Record only administrative conditions supplied by an authorized source. Do not translate clinical criteria into improvised categories.

Restricted means only a defined subset of inquiries is considered after an additional recorded gate, such as a limited program, payer relationship, or authorized screening path. Paused is temporary and needs a next-review date or triggering event. Closed means the source reports no consideration of the defined inquiry type. Unknown means there is no usable current evidence, sources conflict, or contact attempts have not resolved status.

These values describe a time-bound intake posture, not guaranteed appointment supply. A provider can be open when checked and have no remaining slot when a person calls. The register should expose verified-at and effective-at timestamps.

## Three reliability dimensions

Freshness asks how recently the controlling source was observed. Report age distribution rather than declaring every value current under one arbitrary threshold. Different states may warrant different review triggers; temporary pauses, for example, need follow-up tied to their expected end.

Source agreement compares the register with a prespecified reference collected independently. If provider office, centralized intake, payer directory, and internal schedule disagree, record pairwise agreement and conflict. Do not invent a gold standard where none exists.

Operational confirmation tests whether the stated referral pathway was reachable and whether the source confirmed the scoped state. A working phone number is not panel confirmation. Likewise, a confirmed panel response does not prove that directory contact details are broadly accurate.

## Reviewer calibration

Create a guide with examples for every state and restriction field. Two reviewers independently classify a calibration subset from the same evidence packet. Report raw agreement and disagreements by field. Preserve original scores before adjudication.

If reviewers disagree because the source wording is ambiguous, the data dictionary needs revision. If they disagree because evidence conflicts, the correct state may be unknown rather than a forced consensus. Repeat calibration after material rule changes.

## Measures

State completeness is relationships with a permitted panel state divided by eligible relationships, but publish the unknown count because unknown is valid data rather than confirmed availability. Evidence completeness is records with source, observed date, effective date when supplied, and verifier divided by sampled records.

Freshness distribution should show counts in declared age bands. Agreement should be reported for exact state and for individual restrictions. Reached-source rate uses completed contact protocol as its denominator. Stale-to-corrected time starts at a qualifying discrepancy and ends when the register records an approved update.

For referral usefulness, report leads generated from records later contradicted by the source. Keep same-day capacity changes separate from clearly stale register values when evidence permits. Counts matter because small samples can make percentages unstable.

## Bias and failure modes

Records easiest to verify may be systematically different from unreachable providers. Report nonresponse rather than dropping it. Staff may preferentially update open states, leaving closed or paused records stale. Bulk timestamps can reflect migration instead of verification. Free-text restrictions may appear complete but resist consistent searching.

Other failures include status inherited across locations, future effective dates applied early, a pause with no review trigger, and open used to mean contracted rather than accepting inquiries. Audit event history to determine whether overwrites concealed frequent changes.

## Privacy and governance

A panel study usually needs professional and operational data, not client information. Remove referral narratives from the analytic file. Restrict direct contact details where appropriate, use individual accounts, and document exports. Name the register owner, source owner, reviewer, and discrepancy adjudicator.

## Limitations

Status can change immediately after verification. Source contacts may use different definitions, and a sample may miss rare restrictions. Nonresponse creates uncertainty that cannot be solved statistically without assumptions. Reliability at one practice does not generalize to another. Even a fresh, confirmed open state does not prove clinical suitability, coverage, affordability, or appointment completion.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is open panel a yes-or-no field?

Usually it is incomplete without relationship, conditions, source, and time. A binary export may be necessary, but the richer source record should remain available.

### How should conflicting sources be handled?

Preserve each observation and apply a declared, field-specific authority rule. If no rule resolves the conflict, use unknown and assign follow-up.

### Does a stale status count as incorrect?

Not automatically. Stale means its evidence age exceeds a declared review rule. Correctness requires an appropriate reference observation.

### Can panel reliability measure access to care?

It measures one administrative input to access. It does not capture all capacity, preference, transportation, coverage, fit, or completion factors.

## Conclusion

A reliable panel register represents scoped relationships, conditions, and time. Freshness, agreement, and operational confirmation should be measured separately. Unknown states and nonresponse are findings, not records to discard. This approach supports more honest referral routing without turning availability evidence into a promise.
