Proxy access allows one person to use portal functions connected with another person's record. The operational risk is not limited to initial activation. Authority can be limited, changed, disputed, or revoked, while technical permissions may persist across messaging, scheduling, documents, and billing. This brief studies whether each access state can be traced to an authorized decision without deciding what authority is legally sufficient.

## Scope and unit of analysis

Use one proxy relationship version as the observation unit. A new version begins whenever scope, authority basis, status, or effective dates change. Link versions under a stable relationship ID. This avoids overwriting an earlier grant when permissions are narrowed or terminated.

The study covers request receipt, identities, asserted relationship, authority evidence, approved scope, activation, periodic review when locally required, revocation, and technical propagation. It excludes ordinary direct-user accounts and system tests, but their counts should appear in exclusions.

## Decision record and source hierarchy

A proposed decision record has seven fields: subject identity evidence, proxy identity evidence, authority source, approved function scope, decision owner, effective interval, and technical account reference. Each value needs a source pointer and capture time. A free-text relationship label alone is not authority evidence.

> **Liftable statistic:** This editorial framework proposes 7 local fields for a proxy decision record. Seven is a control count, not a legal sufficiency test, federal minimum, or guarantee that access is appropriate.

Document source precedence before review. For example, an authorized decision record may govern the approved scope, while the identity service governs account identity and the portal configuration governs actual permissions. When sources disagree, record a conflict. Do not silently make the technical setting the decision of record.

## Mapping decision to technical permission

Build a function matrix for view, download, message, schedule, bill, amend profile, and any other portal capability. Compare approved scope with enabled scope for each sampled relationship. Record under-permission and over-permission separately because they have different consequences.

Activation evidence should include actor, timestamp, account, decision version, and configuration result. Shared credentials defeat attribution and should not be treated as proxy access. The proxy should use a distinct account linked to the relationship according to the portal design.

Revocation requires its own test. Record request or triggering event, authorized decision, system update, propagation result, and residual sessions or tokens where observable. A status changed in an administrative table is not proof that every function stopped working.

## Measures

Decision traceability equals sampled relationship versions with all applicable source pointers divided by versions reviewed. Scope agreement equals function permissions agreeing with the approved matrix divided by applicable function checks. Report relationships and function checks as separate denominators.

Activation interval runs from authorized approval to technical enablement. Revocation interval runs from authorized termination to confirmed technical disablement. Avoid combining them because system paths and risk differ. State the time zone and how outages or batch processing are handled.

Unknown authority, pending review, active, suspended, revoked, expired, and conflicting evidence are useful distinct states. Completeness only means required fields are populated with permitted values. It does not prove the underlying decision was legally correct.

## Sampling and reproducibility

Sample new grants, changed scopes, routine active relationships, expirations, and revocations. Include different portal functions and request channels. Freeze the query, extraction time, portal version, and data dictionary. For a calibration subset, two reviewers should independently determine whether each source pointer is usable and whether each permission matches recorded scope.

Retain initial ratings and adjudication. If reviewers cannot apply a field consistently, revise the definition before scoring the full sample. Store study IDs instead of names and limit screenshots because portal pages can expose unrelated sensitive information.

## Failure modes and response

Common failures include relationship labels without source evidence, activation before approval, scope copied from a prior relationship, revoked access remaining active in messaging, and a new portal migration losing the decision reference. Minor status changes may also reset effective dates and conceal relationship history.

Choose remediation by mechanism. Add required source references for provenance gaps, role templates for configuration drift, propagation tests for revocation failures, and migration reconciliation for orphaned relationships. Potential inappropriate access should follow the designated privacy and security process, not wait for research publication.

## Limitations

The review observes stored records and testable functions, not every use of access. Some portals cannot expose token or session state. Authority requirements vary by age, relationship, jurisdiction, and circumstances. A technically accurate permission may still rest on an incorrect authorized decision, while a missing link may reflect migration rather than an improper grant. Small samples may miss rare combinations. This method does not certify compliance or determine legal authority.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a family relationship enough to approve proxy access?

This method does not answer that legal or policy question. Record the asserted relationship and route authority evaluation to the designated authorized role.

### Should active proxy access be reviewed periodically?

Follow the approved local policy and applicable guidance. If review is required, capture the due date, reviewer, evidence considered, and resulting relationship version.

### What proves revocation?

Use evidence from each applicable technical function, not only an administrative status. Define whether session termination and downloaded copies are within the test scope.

### Can one completeness score summarize the control?

It can summarize documentation under a declared rule, but it should not replace scope-agreement and revocation tests or imply that decisions were legally correct.

## Conclusion

Traceable proxy administration links a versioned decision to specific portal functions and effective dates. Separate identity, authority, approval, configuration, and revocation evidence makes discrepancies observable. The method supports careful correction while leaving authority determinations with qualified and authorized personnel.
