[P4 | Normal] This SOP covers the foundational changes that should be made to every newly created website before we consider the initial optimization complete.
[P5 | Normal] The goal is not to make every website identical.
[P6 | Normal] The goal is to make sure every website has:
[P7 | Normal] A strong structure

[P9 | Normal] Strong calls to action
[P10 | Normal] Relevant services
[P11 | Normal] Professional visuals
[P12 | Normal] Content that does not look obviously AI-generated
[P13 | Normal] A clear path for the visitor to become a lead
[P15 | Heading1] 1. Model the Website After Stealth Agents
[P16 | Normal] Use Stealth Agents as the general model for how our service websites should be structured and presented.
[P17 | Normal] Pay attention to:
[P18 | Normal] How quickly the website explains what the company does
[P19 | Normal] Placement of calls to action
[P20 | Normal] Navigation
[P21 | Normal] Service presentation
[P22 | Normal] Homepage structure
[P23 | Normal] Conversion flow
[P24 | Normal] How information is broken into sections
[P25 | Normal] How visitors are moved toward contacting or hiring us
[P26 | Normal] Do NOT copy Stealth Agents word-for-word.
[P27 | Normal] Use it as the structural and conversion model, then adapt the messaging, services, branding, and visuals to the specific industry.
[P29 | Heading1] 2. Make Sure the CTA Is Above the Fold
[P30 | Normal] Every website needs a clear call to action above the fold.
[P31 | Heading2] What Is "The Fold"?
[P32 | Normal] The fold is everything a visitor can see when the website first loads BEFORE they have to scroll.
[P33 | Normal] This is the most important part of the homepage.
[P34 | Normal] Within approximately five seconds, a visitor should understand:
[P35 | Normal] What the company does
[P36 | Normal] Who the company helps
[P37 | Normal] What service is being offered
[P38 | Normal] What they should do next
[P39 | Normal] The above-the-fold section should normally contain:
[P40 | Normal] A clear H1
[P41 | Normal] A short supporting statement
[P42 | Normal] A primary CTA
[P43 | Normal] A relevant visual or illustration
[P44 | Normal] Important credibility information when available
[P45 | Heading3] Avoid Generic Headlines
[P46 | Normal] BAD:
[P47 | Normal] "Transform Your Business With Innovative Solutions"
[P48 | Normal] BETTER:
[P49 | Normal] "Hire a Virtual Assistant for Your Practice Management Company"
[P50 | Normal] The headline should immediately tell the visitor what we offer.
[P52 | Heading3] CTA Examples
[P53 | Normal] Find Your VA
[P54 | Normal] Hire a Virtual Assistant
[P55 | Normal] Get Started
[P56 | Normal] Talk to Our Team
[P57 | Normal] Schedule a Consultation
[P58 | Normal] The primary action should be obvious without scrolling.

[P61 | Normal] Use Getillustrations or another approved illustration source whenever appropriate.
[P62 | Normal] The goal is to make the website look intentionally designed rather than automatically generated.
[P63 | Normal] Prioritize:
[P64 | Normal] Professional illustrations
[P65 | Normal] Icons
[P66 | Normal] Industry-specific illustrations
[P67 | Normal] Process graphics
[P68 | Normal] Relevant screenshots
[P69 | Normal] Software visuals
[P70 | Normal] Diagrams
[P71 | Normal] Other visuals that help explain the service
[P72 | Normal] Avoid:
[P73 | Normal] Random AI-generated people
[P74 | Normal] Fake AI office teams
[P75 | Normal] Generic handshake photos
[P76 | Normal] Random laptop and coffee photos
[P77 | Normal] Unrelated stock photography
[P78 | Normal] Images that exist only to fill space
[P79 | Normal] BLURRY IMAGES
[P80 | Normal] Every image should have a reason for being there.
[P83 | Heading1] 4. GET AWAY FROM LOOKING LIKE AI
[P84 | Normal] This is one of the most important parts of the optimization process.
[P85 | Normal] The finished website should NOT immediately look like it was generated by ChatGPT or an AI website builder.
[P86 | Normal] Look for and remove:
[P87 | Normal] Generic AI headlines
[P88 | Normal] Generic corporate language
[P89 | Normal] Repetitive sections
[P90 | Normal] Excessive use of three-card layouts
[P91 | Normal] Sections that all have exactly the same structure
[P92 | Normal] Paragraphs that are all approximately the same length
[P93 | Normal] Too many adjectives
[P94 | Normal] Generic benefits
[P95 | Normal] Generic FAQs
[P96 | Normal] Fake-looking AI imagery
[P97 | Normal] Unnecessary filler
[P98 | Normal] Statements that could apply to almost any company
[P99 | Normal] Avoid common AI language such as:
[P100 | Normal] "Unlock the power of..."
[P101 | Normal] "Elevate your business..."
[P102 | Normal] "In today's fast-paced world..."
[P103 | Normal] "Tailored solutions for your unique needs..."
[P104 | Normal] "Take your business to the next level..."
[P105 | Normal] Be SPECIFIC instead.
[P106 | Normal] BAD:
[P107 | Normal] "Our skilled virtual assistants provide tailored solutions designed to help your business succeed."
[P108 | Normal] BETTER:
[P109 | Normal] "Your VA can answer tenant questions, coordinate maintenance requests, update practice listings, follow up on rent payments, and keep AppFolio organized."
[P110 | Heading2] Simple AI Check
[P111 | Normal] Ask yourself:
[P112 | Normal] "Could I put this exact paragraph on 100 completely different websites?"
[P113 | Normal] If the answer is YES, rewrite it.
[P114 | Normal] The content should sound like it was written specifically for someone working in that industry.

[P165 | Normal] "What am I supposed to do now?"
[P166 | Normal] The primary CTA should normally appear:
[P167 | Normal] Above the fold
[P168 | Normal] Somewhere within the page
[P169 | Normal] Near the bottom of the page
[P170 | Normal] Examples:
[P171 | Normal] Hire a Virtual Assistant
[P172 | Normal] Find Your VA
[P173 | Normal] Get Started
[P174 | Normal] Talk to Our Team

[P177 | Normal] Do not give the visitor ten different actions to choose from.
