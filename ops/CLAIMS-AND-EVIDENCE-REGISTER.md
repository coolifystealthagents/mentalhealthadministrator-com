# Claims and Evidence Register

## Purpose

This register controls public claims for Mental Health Administrator. It prevents capability language from becoming an unsupported promise about clinical care, security, privacy, insurance outcomes, credentials, or legal status.

A service description is evidence of intended scope, not evidence of customer results. No testimonial, customer count, performance statistic, certification, partnership, award, or outcome may be published without retained, reviewable support and approval from the responsible owner.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Evidence | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | No retained support | Do not publish as fact |
| E1 | Internal service specification, template, or process record | Describe what the service is designed to do, using bounded language |
| E2 | Dated first-party operational record that can be checked | Make a narrow factual statement about that record and period |
| E3 | Independent primary source or authoritative public record | Accurately summarize the source with date, link, and limitations |
| E4 | Qualified independent assessment covering the exact claim and current scope | Publish only the assessed wording, scope, date, and limitations |

## Citation record required for external factual claims

For each external claim, retain the exact quotation or data point, source title, issuing organization, canonical URL, publication or revision date, access date, page or section, claim owner, approved wording, and next review date. A search result excerpt, AI answer, marketing page, or unsourced summary is not sufficient evidence.

## Publication rules

1. Use capability language such as can support, tracks, organizes, or routes when describing the designed service.
2. Do not turn an activity into an outcome. Tracking a claim does not prove payment, and tracking an application does not prove enrollment.
3. Keep payer-reported and client-supplied information attributed to its source.
4. State meaningful limitations next to the claim, not only in a distant disclaimer.
5. Remove a claim when its evidence expires, its scope changes, or its source cannot be reproduced.
6. Do not imply HIPAA compliance, a BAA, certification, clinical review, or legal approval through icons, badges, metadata, testimonials, or vague trust language.

## Review cadence

The editorial owner reviews this register whenever service scope, vendors, contracts, security controls, source material, or public copy changes. A quarterly review checks links, dates, limitations, and continued evidence availability. Unresolved claims default to E0 and remain unpublished.
